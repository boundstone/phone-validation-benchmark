# Volunteer consent — Boundstone Benchmark № 001 (category C)

This is the written-consent template referenced by METHODOLOGY.md §3. A volunteer
consents by e-signature or by email reply that quotes and affirms it. Keep every
consent record dated and privately archived, mapped to its dataset row.

---

**Who is collecting this:** Quantum Digital Plus Pty Ltd, trading as Boundstone
(boundstone.io). Contact: hello@boundstone.io.

**What we will do with your phone number:** run it through four commercial
phone-validation APIs — 1Lookup, Trestle, Twilio Lookup, and NumVerify — and record
what each service *claims* about it (whether it's valid, its line type, its carrier,
and possibly the name their records associate with it). **We will never call or text
your number.** The APIs are only asked what they believe about it.

**What we store:** your number, the carrier and line type you attest to (including
your attestation screenshot, redacted to carrier name + line type), and the four
vendors' responses. Stored privately.

**What gets published:** *never* your number and *never* any name. The public
benchmark shows only an anonymous row ID and whether each vendor's claim matched
your attestation. Your attestation screenshot is never published.

**Why:** to measure and publicly report the accuracy of these validation services.
The methodology is frozen and public at boundstone.io/methodology.

**Legal basis (if you are in the EU/UK):** your explicit consent, freely given and
specific to this use.

**Retention and withdrawal:** we keep your row for the benchmark series (quarterly
re-runs) unless you withdraw. Withdraw any time by emailing hello@boundstone.io —
your row is deleted from the private dataset and excluded from all future runs.

**Auditor access:** a neutral third party may review the full private dataset under
a confidentiality agreement to verify our results.

**Voluntariness:** participation is voluntary. Any incentive offered (e.g. via a
research panel) is stated at recruitment and doesn't depend on what the vendors
return about your number.
